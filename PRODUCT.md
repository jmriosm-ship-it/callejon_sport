# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React 19 + Vite (already in place, plain CSS in `src/index.css`).

## Users

Young men, 16 to 30, in Colombia. They find the store on Instagram, check the site on their phone, and close the sale by WhatsApp. They want urban outfits: t-shirts, caps, jeans, bermudas, sneakers, hoodies, sportswear.

## Product Purpose

Callejón Store is an urban clothing store with a physical shop. The site shows the collection and sends the visitor to WhatsApp to order (confirm size, color, availability, then pay and receive by shipment). Success is a visitor who messages the store or visits the shop.

## Positioning

Exclusive brands and pieces, a shop with its own atmosphere (black ceiling, gray walls, wooden floor), personal service over WhatsApp, and fair prices and quality. Another store could copy the clothes, not the shop's room and its people.

## Operating Context

Orders happen by WhatsApp. Shipping covers all of Colombia (carrier chosen by the customer). Social presence on Instagram (@callej0n_store). There is no cart or online checkout.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Static catalog browsing by category, WhatsApp deep links with pre-written messages, shop info (address, hours), shipping and payment info. Undecided facts: WhatsApp number, shop address and hours, real payment methods, city, product photos and prices. Do not invent them.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Callejón Store. Logo: hexagon with the letters CS and the wordmark CALLEJÓN STORE, supplied in black and white (currently with a Christmas hat, a clean version may follow). The original logo is gold on red; the user asked for black and white.

## Evidence on Hand

One photo of the shop interior (`src/assets/local.webp`) and the logo (`src/assets/logo.png`). No product photos yet; absence must not be faked with stock imagery. No prices, no testimonials.

## Product Principles

- Urban first: it should read as streetwear in one glance, not as a generic shop template.
- The shop is the proof: the real room and real people beat stock imagery.
- WhatsApp is the checkout: every product-like element leads to a message.
- Mobile first: the audience arrives from Instagram on a phone.
- Honest placeholders: unknown facts stay visibly pending until the owner supplies them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Spanish (Colombia). Readable on small phones and on slow data. Respect reduced motion.
